**Status: Abandoned**

Son of Olympus was a real-time strategy project I worked on for a while. It’s based on Greek mythology and was heavily inspired by *Age of Mythology*, which is still one of my favorite RTS games.

I used the [RTS Engine](https://gamedevspice.com/assets/rts-engine/) asset to handle the core mechanics. It saved me a ton of time on the basic RTS logic, so I could focus more on the unit design and the environment.
